How to play

Tic-Tac-Toe is played on a 3×3 grid. Two players take turns marking cells with X and O — the first to line up three marks in a row, column, or diagonal wins. Click Play vs Friend to get a shareable link for real-time multiplayer, or Play vs AI to practice solo against three difficulty levels (Easy, Medium, Hard).
