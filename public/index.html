Play Tic-Tac-Toe Online — Free & Multiplayer

Tic-Tac-Toe Online is a free, browser-based version of the classic three-in-a-row game (also known as noughts and crosses). Challenge a friend to a real-time online multiplayer match by sharing a link, or play solo against the computer AI. There is no sign-up and no download — it plays instantly on desktop and mobile.

How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe Online

  1. Choose a mode — Play vs Friend for online multiplayer, or Play vs AI for a solo game.
  2. Invite a friend — for multiplayer, share the game link that is created. You both join the same board.
  3. Take turns — players alternate placing X and O on the 3×3 grid.
  4. Get three in a row — line up three of your marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

Features

  • Real-time online multiplayer — play a friend live by sharing a link.
  • Single-player vs AI — practice against the computer any time.
  • Free and instant — no account, no install, no download required.
  • Plays anywhere — works in any modern browser on desktop, phone, or tablet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Tic-Tac-Toe Online free to play?

Yes. It is completely free with no sign-up, no ads, and no download — just open the page and play.

Can I play Tic-Tac-Toe online with a friend?

Yes. Click Play vs Friend to create a game, then share the link. You both play in real time on the same board.

Can I play Tic-Tac-Toe against the computer?

Yes. Choose Play vs AI to play a solo game against the computer opponent right in your browser.

Do I need to download or install anything?

No. It runs entirely in your web browser on desktop and mobile — there is nothing to download or install.
